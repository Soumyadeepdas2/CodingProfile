# GFG. Half Diamond Star Pattern
**Platform:** GeeksforGeeks
**Difficulty:** N/A
**Topic:** pattern-printing

## Problem Statement
Given an integer n, print the Half Diamond Star Pattern using * characters as shown in the examples.

Examples:

Input: 5
Output:
* 
* * 
* * * 
* * * * 
* * * * *
* * * *
* * *
* *
*
Input: 3
Output:
* 
* * 
* * * 
* *
*

Constraints:
1 ≤ n ≤ 100
